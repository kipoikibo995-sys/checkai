When I started the channel I honestly didn't think anyone would watch. Most nights I was up until 2am going through quotes, reading them out loud, and tossing anything that sounded too cheesy.

The first video got 37 views. Thirty-seven! Probably ten of those were me and my little brother.

Then someone left a comment, something like "this one got me through the week." I just sat there staring at it for a while. That's kind of when I decided to keep going, even if it's slow. I only post a quote if I actually believe it.

The channel's a bit bigger now, but I still do it the same way. If you've got a quote you love, send it over. I read every single one, I promise.
