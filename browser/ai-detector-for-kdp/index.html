Why KDP authors check their manuscripts

When you publish or republish a book, KDP asks whether it contains AI-generated content. Many authors now work with AI in some way, whether for brainstorming, outlining, editing or translation, and with ghostwriters or co-authors whose process they cannot fully see. A detector does not answer the KDP question for you, but it helps you spot the passages worth a second look:

  • Ghostwritten or outsourced chapters. If you paid for writing, a check is a quick way to see whether some sections read like raw AI output before your name goes on the cover.
  • Your own AI-assisted drafts. If you used AI to draft and then rewrote, a check shows which sections still read like the original output and may deserve another pass in your own voice.
  • Reader trust. Readers leave reviews when a book feels generic. The phrases AI overuses, such as "a testament to", "delve into" or "in today's fast-paced world", are exactly what readers notice.

What KDP asks about AI content

KDP's content guidelines ask publishers to tell Amazon when a book contains AI-generated text, images or translations. They describe AI-assisted work differently: if you created the content yourself and used AI tools to edit, refine, error-check or brainstorm, KDP treats that as AI-assisted. Where exactly a heavily edited AI draft falls is something authors and guides disagree about, so read the rules in Amazon's own words before you decide.

This page is general information, not legal or policy advice. The rules can change: always check Amazon's current KDP Content Guidelines before you publish.

A detector score is not part of that decision. KDP asks how the content was made, which you know better than any tool. Use the checker to find passages you want to rewrite, not to decide what to declare.

How to check a manuscript with Koji AI Checker

  1. Work chapter by chapter. The AI model reads up to about 512 tokens (roughly 350 to 400 English words) at a time, so paste one scene or a section of 150 to 400 words per check. Long chapters are best checked in a few parts.
  2. Turn on the AI model. On a computer, choose the Accurate model (a one-time download of about 730 MB that stays in your browser). On a phone, the Lite model (about 275 MB) is suggested.
  3. Read the reasons, not only the number. The result lists the buzzwords and stock transitions it found and highlights them in your text, so you can see what to change.
  4. Rewrite in your own voice and check again. Specific details, opinions, varied sentence lengths and natural dialogue are what make prose read as human.

What a detector cannot tell you

No AI detector is proof, ours included. In our tests on 114 held-out answers from the public HC3 dataset (people vs. ChatGPT), the Accurate model was right 98% of the time. Newer AI models write less predictably: on a small set of 11 texts mostly from 2026 models, it caught 6. Heavily edited or paraphrased AI text is harder still. Formal, plain prose by a person can occasionally look AI-like. Fiction with lots of dialogue usually reads as human, and so does a strong personal voice.

So treat the score as a pointer to passages that deserve attention, never as evidence against a ghostwriter, editor or co-author on its own. Ask for drafts, outlines or version history if you need to know how a text was written.

Why privacy matters for unpublished books

Many online detectors send your text to their servers, and some keep it. For an unpublished manuscript that is a real risk. Koji AI Checker runs the language model inside your own browser: your chapters are never uploaded, and the only thing saved is a short list of recent checks in your own browser, which you can clear at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Does KDP use an AI detector on my book?

Amazon does not publish how it reviews books, so we cannot say. What KDP asks is that you tell it about AI-generated content when you publish. A detector helps you review your own text; it does not replace that disclosure.

Can I check a whole book at once?

Check it in parts. The AI model reads up to about 350 to 400 words at a time, so one scene or section per check gives the most useful result, and the highlights show you exactly where to look.

Is my manuscript uploaded anywhere?

No. The check, including the AI model, runs inside your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

Does it work for books in other languages?

It is built for English. German is available as an experimental option with much lower accuracy. Other languages are not supported.
