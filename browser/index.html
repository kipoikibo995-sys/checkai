      How does this work?
      • Writing-style signals. AI tends to write sentences of similar length, lean on buzzwords like "delve" or "seamless", open sentences with "Moreover" or "Furthermore", and avoid contractions and personal voice. These six signals run right in your browser.
      • Language model (full app only). When the app runs with its server (python -m aicheck serve or the Hugging Face Space), two Qwen2.5 models score how predictable each word is using the Binoculars method. AI text is unusually predictable. The model counts for 70% of the score and also flags individual sentences.
      • It is an estimate. Formulaic human writing can look like AI, and AI text edited by hand can pass as human. Short texts under 120 words are unreliable. Never use a single score as proof.
      • Second opinion. Compare with GPTZero or Copyleaks. If tools disagree, nobody knows for sure.
